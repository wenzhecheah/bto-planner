# BTO in Singapore: research notes for the BTO Planner

_Compiled 6 Nov 2026, mostly from HDB's own pages (sources at the bottom). The rules change several times a year, so check these numbers again before relying on them._

---

## 1. What a BTO is

A **Build-To-Order (BTO)** flat is a new, subsidised HDB flat. You apply for it before it is built, and it is only built once enough units are taken up. Buyers apply in a **sales exercise**. There are about 3 a year: Feb, Jun/Jul and Oct. In 2026 the last one was moved to **Nov** to fit the new income ceilings. A computer ballot decides your queue position, and you pick your unit when your queue number comes up. The flat is usually completed **3–4 years later**. "Shorter Waiting Time" (SWT) projects take under 3 years.

The sales exercises now run alongside a **Sale of Balance Flats (SBF)** exercise. SBF sells leftover or repossessed units, often ones that are already completed.

**Next exercise:** Feb 2027. About 4,300 BTO flats in Bishan, Tampines, Tengah and Toa Payoh, plus about 5,000 SBF flats. **To be safe, submit your HFE documents by 15 Dec 2026.**

## 2. The end-to-end journey (timeline)

| # | Stage | When | What you pay | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | **HFE letter** (HDB Flat Eligibility) | ≥1 month before launch (HDB sets a cut-off date) | — | Required to apply. Sets your eligibility, grant amount and HDB loan amount upfront. If your income or household changes, you must cancel it and reapply. |
| 1 | **Apply** in the 8-day window | Launch week | $10 admin fee (non-refundable) | One flat type in one town per exercise. It is a ballot, not first-come-first-served, so there is no need to rush. Watch the live application rates. |
| 2 | **Ballot result** | Within about 2 months | — | You get a queue number. If it is within the supply, you can't join other exercises until your booking appointment. |
| 3 | **Book a flat** | Weeks to months after the result | **Option fee:** $500 (2-rm Flexi), $1,000 (3-rm), $2,000 (4-rm+) | You must attend in person. Optional Component Scheme items (flooring, doors, sanitary fittings) **can only be chosen here**. Request the bank Letter of Offer after booking if you're taking a bank loan. |
| 4 | **Sign Agreement for Lease (AFL)** | Within 9 months of booking | **Downpayment, part 1, plus stamp duty and legal fees** | Payable by CPF OA (including the grant) and/or cash, depending on the loan type (table in §6). |
| 5 | Wait for construction | About 3–4 years | — | Track the Probable Completion Date (PCD) on MyHDB. HDB checks your finances again ~3 months before completion and may cut the loan. |
| 6 | **Key collection** | Completion | **Downpayment, part 2.** The loan is disbursed. Fees and insurance are due. | Fire insurance is compulsory with an HDB loan. HPS is compulsory if CPF pays the instalments. You must collect within 1 month of HDB's notice, or pay daily interest. |
| 7 | Move in | Within 6 months of keys | Instalments start on the 1st of the 2nd month | Everyone listed must live there. Fiancé couples must solemnise within 3 months. Existing flat owners must sell within 6 months. |
| 8 | **MOP** | 5 yrs (Standard) / 10 yrs (Plus/Prime) | — | You can't sell, rent out the whole flat, or buy private property before the MOP ends. |

## 3. Eligibility (couples/families, the main case)

- **Citizenship:** the applicant must be an SC, plus at least one other SC or SPR. A household of 1 SC + SPR(s) pays a **$10,000 premium**, refunded as a Citizen Top-Up later (when the spouse becomes an SC or you have an SC child). Households of PRs only cannot buy BTO.
- **Age:** at least 21. **Singles:** at least 35, and only a **2-room Flexi** (in Standard, Plus or Prime projects).
- **Household schemes:** fiancé/fiancée, married couple, parents with children, multi-generation (3Gen), orphaned siblings. An SC with a non-resident spouse must be at least 35 for a new flat.
- **Income ceiling** (average gross monthly household income over the 12 months ending **2 months before** the HFE application). Raised for HFE applications made **from 24 Aug 2026**:
  - Families: **$16,000** (previously $14,000)
  - Extended/multi-gen families: **$24,000**
  - Singles (35+): **$8,000** (previously $7,000)
  - 2-room Flexi: $8,000. Some 3-room projects are capped at $8,000. The launch press release says which.
- **Property:** you must not own any private residential property, local or overseas, and must not have sold one in the **last 30 months**. One non-residential property is allowed. Existing HDB owners must sell within 6 months of key collection.
- **Previous subsidies:** you are a second-timer if you already had one subsidy (you pay a resale levy, in cash). With two subsidies you can't buy from HDB at all, except seniors on short-lease flats.

## 4. Standard / Plus / Prime (from the Oct 2024 launch onwards)

| | Standard | Plus | Prime |
|---|---|---|---|
| Location | Everywhere else | Choicer (MRT, amenities, waterfront) | Choicest (central) |
| Extra subsidy | — | More | Most |
| **Subsidy recovery when you sell** | None | Yes, about **6–9%** in recent launches | Yes, about **11–12%** |
| **MOP** | 5 years | 10 years | 10 years |
| Rent out the whole flat after MOP | Yes | **Never** | **Never** |
| Rent out spare rooms | Yes (3-rm+) | Yes | Yes |
| Resale buyers | Open | Tighter conditions | BTO-like eligibility rules |

Subsidy recovery is a % of the **resale price or valuation, whichever is higher**. Recent rates: Feb 2026: Tampines Nova 6%, Redhill Peaks 12%. Nov 2026: Merpati Meadow 8%, Bayshore Sails/Oasis 9%, Toa Payoh Hillside 11%.

> **Planner insight:** a Plus/Prime flat that is cheaper today has a hidden exit cost. A $1M resale at 9% recovery hands HDB $90k. It also locks you in for 10 years with no option to rent out the whole flat.

## 5. Ballot mechanics and your odds

**Ballot chances** (each "chance" is roughly an extra entry in the draw):

| Applicant type | Base chances | After 2+ unsuccessful **Standard** BTO tries |
|---|---|---|
| **FT(PMC)**: first-timer with an SC child ≤18 or expecting, or married couple aged ≤40, who never owned property and had no chance to book in the last 5 yrs | **3** | +1 per further try, **capped at 5** |
| Other first-timer families | 2 | +1 per further try, capped at 4 |
| Second-timer families | 1 | — |
| **New from Feb 2027:** FT families with or expecting children | +1 per SC child aged ≤18 | All BTO and SBF |

**Supply quotas:**
- Standard projects: first-timer families get **≥80% of 3-room** and **≥90% of 4-room+** flats.
- Plus/Prime: first-timer families get **≥90%** of 3-room+.
- **FPPS** (first-timer married couples with or expecting a child, or FT(PMC)): up to **40%** of BTO public supply. FT(PMC) applicants get **first priority** for 4-room-or-smaller **Standard** flats.
- **FCS (Proximity)**, for living with or within 4 km of parents/child: up to 30% for FT families. **FCS (Joint Balloting)**: parents and child apply together for 2 units in one project. **TCPS** (3+ children): up to 10%.

**Demand data:**
- Overall application rate: Jun 2026 **3.2×**, Feb 2026 3.2×, Oct 2025 3.7×. The 4-year average is 3.9×.
- The figure that matters is the **first-timer rate for your flat type** (e.g. Jun 2026 Berlayar Rise 4-rm: 3.1× FT vs 22.1× ST). Non-mature or Standard 4-rooms are often **≤1×**, meaning almost a sure thing for first-timers.
- HDB itself advises looking for projects at an application rate of about 1 or lower. Rates are updated several times a day during the window.

**Penalties for walking away:**

| When you cancel or refuse | Money lost | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| During the application window | Nil | — |
| You get a queue number but **don't pick a flat** when units are available | Nil | **Non-selection count.** First-timer families are treated as second-timers for 1 year and their extra ballot chances reset to 0. Singles and second-timers are barred for 1 year. _Waived if ≤10 BTO flats were left to choose from._ |
| After booking, before the AFL | **Option fee** | 1-year bar. Accumulated chances reset. |
| After the AFL, before keys | **5% of the price** | 1-year bar. Stamp duty may be refundable from IRAS. Legal fees are lost. |
| Don't collect the keys | Option fee + 5% + **grants returned with interest** | — |

## 6. Finances

### 6.1 Price
HDB publishes "from" prices per project and flat type at launch, both before and after an assumed grant. Nov 2026 examples: Standard 4-rm from **$312k–$353k**; Plus 4-rm from **$545k–$559k**; Prime Toa Payoh 4-rm from **$558k**. **White flats** (no internal partition walls) are $6k / $8.6k / $9k cheaper for 3- / 4- / 5-room.

### 6.2 Grant: Enhanced CPF Housing Grant (EHG)
For first-timer families. The income ceiling is **$9,000**, which was **not** raised with the BTO ceiling. You need **12 months of continuous employment** ending 2 months before the HFE application, and must be working at the time you apply. The grant is credited to the CPF OA and can only be used for the flat.

| Avg monthly household income | EHG | | Avg monthly household income | EHG |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ≤ $1,500 | $120,000 | | $5,001–5,500 | $55,000 |
| $1,501–2,000 | $110,000 | | $5,501–6,000 | $50,000 |
| $2,001–2,500 | $105,000 | | $6,001–6,500 | $40,000 |
| $2,501–3,000 | $95,000 | | $6,501–7,000 | $30,000 |
| $3,001–3,500 | $90,000 | | $7,001–7,500 | $25,000 |
| $3,501–4,000 | $80,000 | | $7,501–8,000 | $20,000 |
| $4,001–4,500 | $70,000 | | $8,001–8,500 | $10,000 |
| $4,501–5,000 | $65,000 | | $8,501–9,000 | $5,000 |

- **First-timer + second-timer couple:** the EHG (Singles) table uses **half** the household income. The maximum is $60,000 (≤$750 half-income), falling in $250 steps down to $2,500 (at $4,251–4,500).
- The grant is pro-rated if the lease doesn't cover the youngest core member to age 95.
- If the grant exceeds 95% of the price, you still pay ≥5% yourself.
- **Step-Up Grant** ($15k) exists for second-timers moving from a 2-room to a 3-room Standard flat. Not modelled here.

### 6.3 Loan: HDB vs bank

| | **HDB loan** | **Bank loan** |
|---|---|---|
| Interest | **2.6% p.a.** (CPF OA rate + 0.1%, reviewed quarterly) | Floating (SORA-pegged) or fixed. SORA was ~1.26% in Oct 2026, so banks can be cheaper today but rates can rise. |
| LTV | **75%** (pro-rated if the lease doesn't cover the youngest to 95) | 75% (min **5% cash**) or 55% |
| Income cap | $16k families / $8k singles | None |
| Affordability test | **MSR 30%** of gross income, with instalments computed at a **3.0% floor** | **MSR 30%** + **TDSR 55%** (all debts), computed at a **4% floor** |
| Tenure | Shortest of **25 yrs**, **65 − average age**, lease − 20 | Up to 30 yrs. The 75% LTV only applies if the tenure is ≤25 yrs and ends by age 65, otherwise 55% (MAS rule from memory, not re-checked) |
| CPF OA | Must use the OA balance first. May keep **up to $20,000 per person**. | No forced OA drawdown |
| Late penalty | Gentler | Bank terms |
| Switching | Can refinance to a bank later | **Can't go back** to an HDB loan |

### 6.4 Payment schedule (percent of the purchase price)

| Scheme | At AFL signing | At key collection |
|---|---|---|
| **HDB loan / no loan** (normal) | **10%** (CPF/cash) | **15%** + 75% loan |
| **Staggered Downpayment (SDS):** FT or FT+ST couple, HFE application on or before the younger applicant's **30th birthday**, uncompleted flat ≤5-rm. Applied automatically. | **5%** | **20%** + loan |
| **Deferred Income Assessment:** ≥1 applicant aged ≤30, one party a student/NSF or graduated/ORD within 12 months | **2.5%** | **22.5%**. The grant and loan are assessed ~3 months before completion. |
| **Bank loan 75%** | 20% (≥**5% cash**) | 5% |
| **Bank loan 75% + SDS** | 10% (≥5% cash) | 15% |
| **Bank loan 55%** | 20% (≥10% cash) | 25% |

The option fee is deducted from the AFL downpayment.

### 6.5 Fees (all can be paid with CPF OA unless noted)
- **Stamp duty on the AFL** (BSD): 1% of the first $180k, 2% of the next $180k, 3% of the next $640k, 4% above that. Example: $350k gives **$5,200**.
- **Legal fee (purchase)** when HDB acts for you: $0.90/k for the first $30k, $0.72/k for the next $30k, $0.60/k above that. Round up to the dollar, then add 9% GST. Example: $350k gives **$243.07**. Charged the same way on the **loan amount** for the mortgage.
- At keys: **survey fee** ($163.50 for 2-rm, $231.60 for 3-rm, $299.75 for 4-rm, $354.25 for 5-rm). **Lease and mortgage registration** $38.30 each. **Stamp duty on the Deed of Assignment** is 0.4% of the loan, capped at $500.
- **Insurance:** HDB Fire Insurance (through Etiqa, compulsory with an HDB loan). **Home Protection Scheme** (compulsory if CPF pays the instalments, premium payable by CPF).
- **Ongoing after keys:** S&CC, property tax, utilities.
- **Not from HDB, but your biggest cash item:** **renovation and furniture**, which is mostly **cash**. Get real quotes. The planner should treat this as a user input, not a constant.

### 6.6 Worked example (checked against HDB's own sample calculation)
4-room Standard flat at **$350,000**. First-timer couple, combined income **$7,000/month**, HDB loan, no SDS.

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| EHG ($6,501–7,000 band) | **−$30,000** (into the CPF OA) |
| Option fee at booking | $2,000 |
| AFL: 10% downpayment − option fee | $33,000 |
| AFL: stamp duty + legal fee | $5,200 + $243 |
| Keys: 15% downpayment | $52,500 |
| Keys: fees (mortgage legal fee $186, Deed of Assignment stamp duty $500, survey fee $300, registrations $77) | ≈ $1,063 |
| **HDB loan (75%)** | **$262,500** |
| Monthly instalment at 2.6% over 25 yrs | **≈ $1,191** |
| HDB's affordability check (3% floor) | $1,245 ≤ MSR cap $2,100 ✅ |
| Maximum loan MSR would allow at this income | ≈ $442,800 (but the 75% LTV binds first) |
| Total interest over 25 yrs | ≈ $94,800 |

The upfront 25% ($87.5k) plus fees comes from **CPF OA (including the $30k grant) and cash**. The more OA you have, the less cash you need. A couple's CPF contributions (23% of salary going to OA for those under 35) usually cover the $1,191/month instalment, which is what HDB means by "little or no cash".

## 7. What to watch out for

1. **The HFE letter is the real start line.** Apply early (the Feb 2027 doc cut-off is 15 Dec 2026). Any change in income, job or household means cancelling and reapplying, and you can't do that during a launch window.
2. **Income is backward-looking.** Grants use the 12 months ending 2 months before the HFE application. A raise or bonus can push you into a lower EHG band. Employment gaps can disqualify you from the EHG entirely.
3. **The loan is re-checked before keys.** Losing your job or taking on new debts (like a car loan) in the 3–4 year wait can **shrink your HDB loan**, and you make up the gap in cash.
4. **Location affects your odds.** Mature, Plus or Prime estates can be 3–20× oversubscribed. Standard, non-mature 4-rooms are often ≤1× for first-timers. Applying repeatedly to Standard projects builds extra ballot chances. Plus/Prime attempts don't.
5. **Refusing a queue number is costly.** A non-selection count turns you into a second-timer for a year and wipes your accumulated chances. Only apply where you'd actually take a unit.
6. **Count the exit cost of Plus/Prime flats.** That means the 6–12% clawback, a 10-yr MOP, and no whole-flat rental ever. These flats suit people who'll stay long-term, not investors.
7. **Cash vs CPF.** Bank loans need **5% in cash** upfront. Renovation is cash. The option fee is likely cash too (HDB's page doesn't say; check). Keeping $20k in OA means paying more cash elsewhere.
8. **Don't drain CPF blindly.** Every OA dollar used on housing must be refunded **with accrued interest** when you sell. A lower loan is good, but an empty OA leaves no buffer.
9. **Construction delays happen.** Plan rent or a stay with parents around the PCD with slack. Rental and BTO timelines should overlap.
10. **Fiancé scheme:** you must marry within 3 months of key collection. Breaking up after the AFL costs 5% of the price.
11. **Private property:** you need a clean 30-month gap since selling one, including inherited or overseas property.
12. **Optional components and White flats are decided at booking.** You can't add them later.

## 8. What this means for the planner

Three pieces of logic come out of this research:

1. **Eligibility check:** citizenship, age, household scheme, income vs ceiling, property history, first- or second-timer status. These give which flat types and classifications are allowed, and which payment scheme (SDS or Deferred) applies.
2. **Affordability and payment schedule.** Inputs: price, flat type, income, ages, CPF OA balances, cash, loan type. Outputs: EHG; a milestone-by-milestone cash vs CPF breakdown (§6.4 + §6.5); max loan as the lower of LTV, MSR at the 3% floor, and the tenure rule; the monthly instalment; and cash left after renovation.
3. **Ballot and timeline view:** ballot chances (§5), expected dates from launch through HFE cut-off, booking, AFL, keys and MOP end, plus the cost of exiting a Plus/Prime flat (subsidy recovery).

Values that change and should live in **one config table**, not in the code:
- Income ceilings, EHG bands, HDB rate (2.6%), assessment floor (3%), LTV (75%)
- Stamp duty tiers, legal fee tiers, survey fees, option fees
- Subsidy recovery % (per project)
- Launch calendar and HFE cut-offs

Project prices and application rates come out with each launch. HDB publishes them on the Flat Portal and in press releases. I found no public API, so the planner will likely start with manually entered project data.
